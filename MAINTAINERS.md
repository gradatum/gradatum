## Becoming a maintainer

The current process (will tighten as the project matures):

1. **Sustained contribution.** A track record of accepted PRs, reviewed RFCs, or triaged issues across at least three months.
2. **Existing-maintainer nomination.** Any active maintainer may open an issue proposing the contributor for the maintainer role, citing concrete contributions.
3. **Lazy-consensus 14 days.** The proposal is open for 14 days. Silence = acceptance. A sustained objection blocks promotion until resolved.
4. **CLA already on file.** All contributors have signed the [CLA](CLA.md) before any contribution lands; this is pre-condition, not a gate.
5. **Add to this file.** The proposing maintainer adds the new maintainer to the roster in the same PR that closes the nomination issue.

---

## Stepping down

Maintainers may step down at any time by opening a PR removing themselves from this file. The PR is approved by any other maintainer. No discussion required.

If a maintainer is unreachable for **6 months** (no commits, reviews, or messages), the remaining maintainers may move them to "emeritus" status with a single PR.

## Lead maintainer

The lead-maintainer role rotates yearly **once `bus_factor ≥ 3`**. Until then there is no formal lead — decisions go through lazy-consensus among the active maintainer set, with escalation to the public mailing list if consensus is impossible.

See [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md) for the lead-maintainer's tie-breaking role.
