## Why this CLA?

Gradatum's CLA enables the project to:

1. **Stay legally clear** — every contribution has explicit, recorded terms.
2. **Defend against patent litigation** — patent peace clause (Section 3).
3. **Evolve licensing strategy** — Section 2 allows the project to relicense
   if business needs change (e.g., dual-license to fund development, or move
   to AGPL to protect against unfair commercial exploitation).
